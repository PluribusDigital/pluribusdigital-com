We're excited to welcome Jon Carr to Pluribus Digital as our new Chief Growth Officer.

In this role, Jon will define and lead the growth strategy for expanding our federal presence across our Design, Development, and Delivery capabilities, with a focus on three target markets: Health and Benefits, Homeland Security, and Federal Financial agencies.

## Jon's background

Jon brings more than 18 years of experience in the federal market. Before joining Pluribus Digital, he held growth leadership roles at other digital services companies, where he was responsible for driving net-new growth while building brand presence and capabilities within target markets.

Jon holds a bachelor's degree from James Madison University, where he stays involved with the alumni network as Co-Chair for Alumni Engagement with the JMU Federal Dukes.

## What this means for Pluribus Digital

As we continue to grow our work in support of public service missions, Jon's experience connecting federal agencies with the right technical capabilities will help us reach more of the teams that need what we do. Welcome to the team, Jon!
